How to Play

Guess all words in a limited number of tries.

Press 'ENTER' to submit your guess.

Colors indicate the correctness of each letter. For example:

 purple = correct letter and position

 orange = correct letter, wrong position

 grey = wrong letter

Each word will reuse letter(s) from the previous word, starting with 1 and ending with 3.

These letter(s) will show up on your keyboard as present.

The puzzle resets every day at midnight EST (New York)
