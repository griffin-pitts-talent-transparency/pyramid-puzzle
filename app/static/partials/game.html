How to Play

Guess the words in a limited number of tries.

Press Enter to submit your guess.

Colors indicate the correctness of each letter:

 purple = correct letter and position

 orange = correct letter, wrong position

 grey = wrong letter

Each word contains an increasing # of letters from the previous word, starting with 1 letter and moving to 3. Example:

 bad

 ache

 high

 fright

This puzzle will reset every day at 12am EST
